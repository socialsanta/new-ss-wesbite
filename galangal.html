Galangal brand and food photography case study

SERVICE:

BRANDING & PRODUCTION

CATEGORY:

THE PAN ASIAN KITCHEN

Galangal is a pan-Asian kitchen. Its identity pairs a ginger-root mark with a refined serif wordmark in charcoal blue and warm taupe, and its food is shot to match.

ProjectGalangal
CapabilityBranding & Production
CategoryPan-Asian Kitchen

Food photography

Dark marble, bamboo mats and blue-and-white porcelain give every dish a calm, premium stage, with props that echo the brand’s own blue.

Logo design

A hand-drawn ginger root sits above a widely spaced serif wordmark, framed by fine rules and a quiet “The Pan Asian Kitchen” line. It reads as refined, warm and unmistakably Asian without leaning on cliche.

Typography

Secondary fonts for print and media: elegant, classic and clear on business cards, menus and event signage.

  • Stylista Display
  • Poppins Regular Text
  • Blinds Audience Accent

A palette of two calm tones

Charcoal Blue brings depth and quiet confidence, while Warm Taupe adds softness and warmth. Each comes with a set of tints for backgrounds, menus and print.

  • Charcoal Blue
  • Warm Taupe
