Niyaaz brand and production case study

ProjectNiyaaz
CapabilityBranding & Production
LocationBelgaum

Brand in action

From the lit-up signboard to the kraft takeaway bag that says “Taking Belgaum Biryani to the world”, the red wordmark does the talking, and the food is shot warm and close.

Logo design

A flowing, calligraphic wordmark in a rich red, topped with a small diamond and underlined by long curling strokes. It feels rooted and royal, and the tagline “The Original Belgaum Biryani” says exactly what you are getting.

Typography

Avigea gives the wordmark its flowing, handwritten character. Poppins keeps taglines, menus and body copy clean and easy to read.

A palette of red and gold

A deep red brings appetite, heat and heritage, while saffron gold adds warmth, richness and a hint of celebration. Together they feel festive and unmistakably biryani.
